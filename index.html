Hokkaido Travel Essentials & Packing Checklist

Important transit tips, currency recommendations, essential apps, and interactive packing items.

Rail Pass & IC Card

Purchase a 5 or 7-Day JR Hokkaido Rail Pass for intercity transit. Load a Kitaca / Suica / Pasmo card for Sapporo subway rides and convenience store runs.

Currency & Cash

Japanese Yen (JPY). While cards are accepted in cities, carry cash (¥15,000–25,000) for local market stalls in Otaru, buses, and vending machines.

eSIM & Connectivity

Reserve an eSIM or Pocket Wi-Fi before arrival. Mountain passes around Biei, Daisetsuzan, and Noboribetsu require reliable GPS.

Overall Hokkaido 7-Day Travel Circuit

• Days 1-2: Central base in Sapporo with a coastal day excursion to historical Otaru.

• Day 3: Southbound journey to thermal Jigokudani (Noboribetsu) and Lake Toya volcanic caldera.

• Days 4-5: Central countryside exploring Biei Blue Pond, Furano lavender fields, Ningle Terrace, and Asahiyama Zoo.

• Days 6-7: Return transit to Sapporo for city shopping, Tanukikoji, and departure via Chitose Airport.
